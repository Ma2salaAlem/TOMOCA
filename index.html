The Origin

Every coffee plant on earth traces back here.

The arabica plant was discovered growing wild in Ethiopia's highlands, and coffee has anchored daily life here ever since — the buna ceremony still gathers families around roasting beans and slow conversation, a ritual built on unison and respect.

Tomoca was built to honor that origin: single-origin Ethiopian beans, roasted in-house since 1953, and served everywhere from the standing-room espresso bar to the bag you take home.

The Roastery

66+ years of roasting Ethiopian highland beans.

Roasting is a craft that takes training, patience, and time to trust. Tomoca's roasters have worked the same process since 1953 — and the Ethiopian highlands do the rest: rich soil, favorable climate, and a rare low-acidity, full-bodied bean most origins can't match.

130°C – LIGHT

Light Roast

Pulled before any oil reaches the surface, right as the bean fully develops. Our predetermined roast time builds caramelization and body while naturally offsetting acidity.

160°C – MEDIUM

Medium Roast

Full caramelization, subdued acidity, and a light oil sheen on the bean. A deep cup flavor without the sharper edges of a dark roast.

185°C – DARK (BAR)

Dark Roast · Bar

Fully covered in oil, edging toward carbonization. Rich flavor and heavy body dominate as the acidity all but disappears — the roast behind the bar's espresso.
